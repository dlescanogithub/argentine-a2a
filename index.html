ArGENTine

Checklist HITL/blast gate for Diego Lescano (Head/Director AI track).

Send a text brief and optional blast class, tools, and egress. The gate answers only GO, NO_GO, or NEED_HUMAN, plus the failed checks. It does not post, spend, or execute tools. The live gate is deployed at https://argentine-a2a-production.up.railway.app and is not hosted on this page. Local runs stay on localhost.

  • GO — every checklist item passes.
  • NEED_HUMAN — something required for a pass is missing.
  • NO_GO — a check failed, including any failure on a high-blast action.
